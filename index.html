THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Curiosity is the starting point.

I'm Jihan Bhagat, a Computer Science and IT diploma student at SAL Institute of Diploma Studies in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. I enjoy learning by building practical projects — from responsive websites and software experiments to Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, and IoT prototypes. I'm especially interested in how connected devices, automation, networking, Linux, and cybersecurity can solve real problems. AgroHydros is one of the concepts I'm exploring: a smart irrigation system that brings sensing and software together. Away from the keyboard, I enjoy Taekwondo, Kudo, gaming, and 3D printing. My goal is to keep experimenting, document what I learn, and turn useful ideas into working things.
